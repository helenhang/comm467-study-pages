Introduction导论：确定性下的选择

Opportunity sets, indifference curves and the two-period consumption–investment decision under certainty.

Key concepts本章要点

The two-period opportunity setp.4–6两期机会集（预算线）

With income Y₁ and Y₂ and the ability to lend or borrow at rate r, each dollar not consumed in period 1 becomes (1 + r) dollars in period 2. The opportunity set is therefore a straight line with slope −(1 + r) that always passes through the income point (Y₁, Y₂).

第 1 期少花 1 元存起来，第 2 期多花 (1 + r) 元，所以预算线斜率为 −(1 + r)，并经过收入点。

C₂ = Y₂ + (Y₁ − C₁)(1 + r) Max C₁ = Y₁ + Y₂/(1 + r) (present value) Max C₂ = Y₁(1 + r) + Y₂ (future value)

Indifference curves and the optimal choicep.6–8无差异曲线与最优选择

All points on one indifference curve are equally preferred. Curves slope downward (more is preferred to less), are convex to the origin (diminishing marginal rate of substitution), and curves further up and to the right are better. The optimum is where the opportunity set is tangent to the highest attainable indifference curve; in the two-period problem the marginal rate of substitution then equals 1 + r.

曲线向下倾斜、凸向原点，越往右上越好。最优点是预算线与最高无差异曲线的切点，此时边际替代率 = 1 + r。

Tangency condition: MRS = −dC₂/dC₁ (along the indifference curve) = 1 + r

Present value and unequal borrowing/lending ratesp.6–8现值比较与借贷利率不同

In a perfect market, the income stream with the higher present value shifts the whole opportunity set outward, so it is preferred whatever your tastes. If the borrowing rate exceeds the lending rate, the opportunity set kinks at the income point: slope −(1 + rlend) to the left, −(1 + rborrow) to the right.

现值高的收入流让预算线整体外移，无论偏好如何都更好。借款利率高于存款利率时，预算线在收入点处弯折。

1.1

The candy store opportunity set糖果店的机会集

Walking down an unfamiliar street one day, you come across an old-fashioned candy store. They have red hots five for one penny, and rock candy—one small piece for one penny. You decide to purchase some for yourself and your friends, but you find that you have only $1.00 in your pocket. Construct your opportunity set both geometrically and algebraically. Draw in your indifference map (set of indifference curves). Explain why you have drawn your indifference curves as you have drawn them.

Show answer 查看答案
$1.00 = 100 cents. Red hots (H) cost 0.2¢ each; rock candy (K) costs 1¢ each. 0.2H + 1K ≤ 100 ⇔ H + 5K ≤ 500, H ≥ 0, K ≥ 0 Intercepts: all red hots H = 500; all rock candy K = 100; slope dK/dH = −1/5
The opportunity set is the triangle under the red budget line. Grey curves are illustrative indifference curves H·K = constant.红线以下的三角形为机会集，灰线为示例无差异曲线。

The indifference curves slope downward because both candies are desirable: giving up some of one must be compensated with more of the other. They are convex to the origin because the more red hots you already have, the fewer rock candies you will give up for another one (diminishing marginal rate of substitution). Higher curves represent greater satisfaction. With the illustrative preference, the optimum is the tangency point H = 250, K = 50 (50¢ on each).

向下倾斜：两种糖都想要，少一种要用另一种补偿。凸向原点：边际替代率递减。越高的曲线越满意。示例最优点为各花 50 美分。

1.2

Two periods: $20 income, 10% rate两期消费：每期 $20，利率 10%

Let us solve a two-period consumption investment decision similar to the one presented in the text. Assume that you have income equal to $20 in each of two periods. Furthermore, you have the ability to both lend and borrow money at a 10% rate. Draw the opportunity set and your indifference map. Show the optimum amount of consumption in each period.

Show answer 查看答案
C₂ = Y₂ + (Y₁ − C₁)(1 + r) = 20 + (20 − C₁)(1.1) = 42 − 1.1C₁ Max C₁ = 20 + 20/1.1 = 38.18 (borrow all of period-2 income) Max C₂ = 20·1.1 + 20 = 42 (lend all of period-1 income)
Red line: opportunity set C₂ = 42 − 1.1C₁. Blue dot: income point (20, 20). Grey: illustrative indifference curves.红线为机会集，蓝点为收入点，灰线为示例无差异曲线。

The optimum is the tangency between an indifference curve and the opportunity set. With U = C₁C₂: maximize C₁(42 − 1.1C₁) → C₁ = 19.09, C₂ = 21.00, i.e. lend $0.91 in period 1.

最优点为切点。以 U = C₁C₂ 为例，第 1 期贷出 $0.91。

1.5

Which job to choose选哪份工作

An individual has two employment opportunities involving the same work conditions but different incomes. Job 1 yields Y₁ = 50, Y₂ = 30. Job 2 yields Y₁ = 40, Y₂ = 40. Given that markets are perfect and bonds yield 5%, which should be selected?

Show answer 查看答案

With perfect markets you can borrow and lend freely at 5%, so compare present values. The higher present value gives an opportunity set that lies entirely outside the other, so it is preferred regardless of preferences.

市场完美时只比较现值，现值高的机会集完全在外侧。

Choose Job 1 (PV higher by 0.48).

The Characteristics of the Opportunity Set under Risk风险下机会集的特征

Expected return, variance, covariance, correlation, and how diversification reduces portfolio risk.

Key concepts本章要点

Expected return and standard deviationp.43–46期望收益与标准差

The expected return is the probability-weighted average of the possible outcomes; with historical data each period gets equal weight, so it is the simple average. Variance measures how far returns spread around the mean; its square root, the standard deviation, is the book’s measure of risk.

期望收益是按概率加权的平均；历史数据下就是简单平均。标准差是方差的平方根，用来度量风险。

R̄ = Σ Pⱼ Rⱼ σ² = Σ Pⱼ (Rⱼ − R̄)² historical data: Pⱼ = 1/N

Covariance and correlationp.47–50协方差与相关系数

Covariance measures whether two assets move together: positive if they rise and fall together, negative if they move oppositely, zero if unrelated. The correlation coefficient scales covariance to lie between −1 and +1. Assets driven by unrelated factors (rainfall vs. the stock market in 4.1) have zero covariance.

协方差看是否同涨同跌；相关系数把它标准化到 −1 到 +1。受不同因素驱动的资产协方差为 0。

σᵢⱼ = Σ Pⱼ (Rᵢ − R̄ᵢ)(Rⱼ − R̄ⱼ) ρᵢⱼ = σᵢⱼ / (σᵢ σⱼ)

Portfolio expected return and variancep.47–52组合的期望收益与方差

Portfolio expected return is the weighted average of the assets’ expected returns, but portfolio variance is not: it also contains a covariance term for every pair of assets. Because of those terms, combining assets with low correlation gives a portfolio risk below the weighted average of the individual risks.

组合收益是加权平均，组合方差不是：它包含每一对资产的协方差项。相关性低时组合风险更小。

R̄p = Σ Xᵢ R̄ᵢ σp² = Σ Xᵢ² σᵢ² + Σ Σ(i≠j) Xᵢ Xⱼ σᵢⱼ Two assets: σp² = X₁²σ₁² + X₂²σ₂² + 2X₁X₂ρ₁₂σ₁σ₂

Diversification: why risk does not fall to zerop.52–59 · Tables 4.8, 4.9分散化：为什么风险降不到 0

In an equally weighted portfolio of N stocks, the contribution of individual variances shrinks with 1/N and disappears, but the covariance terms approach the average covariance, which cannot be diversified away. The floor on portfolio risk is the average covariance, the market-wide (systematic) risk. Countries differ in how large the average covariance is relative to the average variance, so diversification works better in some markets than others (Table 4.9).

个股方差的贡献按 1/N 消失，协方差项趋近平均协方差，分散不掉。各国市场的平均协方差比例不同，分散化效果也不同。

σp² = (1/N)(σ̄ⱼ² − σ̄ₖⱼ) + σ̄ₖⱼ as N → ∞, σp² → σ̄ₖⱼ

Portfolios offering the highest return for a given risk (and the lowest risk for a given return) form the efficient frontier: the part of the curve from the minimum-variance portfolio upward. Why a single-index model?p.127–128为什么需要单指数模型

Full mean–variance analysis needs a covariance for every pair of stocks: N(N − 1)/2 of them, or 11,175 for 150 stocks. The single-index model assumes stocks are related only through one common factor, the market, so it needs only αᵢ, βᵢ and σₑᵢ for each stock plus R̄ₘ and σₘ²: 3N + 2 inputs in total.

完整分析需要 N(N − 1)/2 个协方差；单指数模型只需要 3N + 2 个输入。

The basic equation and its assumptionsp.128–133基本方程与假设

A stock’s return splits into a market-independent part αᵢ, a market-sensitive part βᵢRₘ, and a random residual eᵢ. By construction the residual has mean zero and is uncorrelated with the market. The key assumption is that residuals of different stocks are uncorrelated, cov(eᵢ, eⱼ) = 0; that is what reduces every covariance to βᵢβⱼσₘ².

收益 = α + β·市场收益 + 残差。关键假设是不同股票的残差互不相关，所以协方差只剩 βᵢβⱼσₘ²。

7.3

Vasicek reduces to proportional weighting when standard errors are equal证明：标准误相同时 Vasicek 是比例加权

Show that the Vasicek technique leads to a simple proportional weighting of the market beta and the stock’s beta if the standard error of all betas is the same.

Show answer 查看答案
Vasicek: βᵢ₂ = [σ²_β̄₁ / (σ²_β̄₁ + σ²_βᵢ₁)] · βᵢ₁ + [σ²_βᵢ₁ / (σ²_β̄₁ + σ²_βᵢ₁)] · β̄₁ If every beta has the same standard error, σ²_βᵢ₁ = σ²_β (a constant). Let w = σ²_β̄₁ / (σ²_β̄₁ + σ²_β) (the same constant for every stock) Then βᵢ₂ = w·βᵢ₁ + (1 − w)·β̄₁

The weight w no longer varies across stocks, so every forecast beta is the same fixed-proportion average of the stock’s own historical beta and the average beta. This has the same form as Blume’s linear adjustment βᵢ₂ = (1 − w)β̄₁ + w·βᵢ₁, with intercept (1 − w)β̄₁ and slope w.

权重 w 对所有股票相同，因此每只股票都是自身 β 与平均 β 的固定比例加权，形式与 Blume 调整一致。

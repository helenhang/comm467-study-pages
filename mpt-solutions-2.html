The Correlation Structure of Security Returns: Multi-index Models and Grouping Techniques证券收益的相关结构：多指数模型与分组方法

Extending the single-index model with more indexes (industry, sector, macro factors) or replacing it with simple averaging models.

Key concepts本章要点

General multi-index modelp.156–159一般多指数模型

Returns depend on several indexes: Ri = ai + bi1I1 + … + biLIL + ci. The model is easiest to use when the indexes are made orthogonal (uncorrelated with each other) and the residuals ci are uncorrelated across stocks. Then every mean, variance and covariance needs only the index statistics and each stock’s b’s.

收益由多个指数决定。把指数正交化（彼此不相关）、残差之间不相关后，均值、方差、协方差都只需要指数的统计量和各股票的 b 系数。

R̄ᵢ = aᵢ + Σₖ bᵢₖ Īₖ σᵢ² = Σₖ bᵢₖ² σ²(Iₖ) + σ²(cᵢ) σᵢⱼ = Σₖ bᵢₖ bⱼₖ σ²(Iₖ) (indexes orthogonal, cov(cᵢ, cⱼ) = 0)

Orthogonalizing correlated indexesp.169–170 (App. A)把相关的指数正交化

If two indexes are correlated, regress the second on the first: I₂* = γ₀ + γ₁I₁ + d. The residual d = I₂* − (γ₀ + γ₁I₁) is uncorrelated with I₁ by construction and becomes the new second index. Substitute back to get new coefficients. With three or more indexes, repeat: regress each new index on all the earlier (already orthogonal) ones.

用第二个指数对第一个回归，残差 d 与 I₁ 不相关，作为新的第二个指数，再代回原式得到新系数。指数更多时依次重复。

Rᵢ = aᵢ* + bᵢ₁*I₁ + bᵢ₂*I₂* + cᵢ and I₂* = γ₀ + γ₁I₁ + I₂ ⇒ Rᵢ = (aᵢ* + bᵢ₂*γ₀) + (bᵢ₁* + bᵢ₂*γ₁)·I₁ + bᵢ₂*·I₂ + cᵢ

Industry index modelsp.159–162行业指数模型

A special two-index model: a market index plus one index for the stock’s own industry, with industry indexes uncorrelated with the market and with each other. Two stocks in the same industry are linked through both the market and their industry index; stocks in different industries are linked only through the market.

市场指数加上各自行业的指数。同一行业的股票通过市场和行业两条渠道相关；不同行业的只通过市场相关。

Same industry j: σᵢₖ = bᵢₘ bₖₘ σₘ² + bᵢⱼ bₖⱼ σ²(Iⱼ) Different industries: σᵢₖ = bᵢₘ bₖₘ σₘ²
Problems8.68.7

Average correlation, mixed and fundamental modelsp.162–169平均相关模型、混合模型与基本面多因子模型

Average (constant) correlation models set every pairwise correlation equal to the historical average (overall, or one average per pair of industries). Mixed models keep the single-index model and model the leftover “extra-market covariance” separately. Fundamental multi-index models (e.g. BARRA-style) use firm characteristics and macro factors as indexes. The book’s main finding: complex models fit past correlations better, but simple models (single-index, overall mean correlation) often forecast future correlations as well or better, because the extra parameters add noise.

平均相关模型：所有相关系数取历史平均。混合模型：单指数模型加上额外的协方差结构。基本面模型：用公司特征和宏观因子作指数。书的结论：复杂模型拟合历史更好，但预测未来时简单模型往往不输，因为多出来的参数带来噪声。

Beta when all correlations are equal所有相关系数相同时的 beta

Given that the correlation coefficient between all securities is the same, call it ρ*, and the assumption of the single-index model is accepted, derive an expression for the beta on any stock in terms of ρ*.

Show answer 查看答案

Under the single-index model, the covariance between two stocks comes only through the market, so each correlation is the product of the two stocks’ correlations with the market:

σᵢⱼ = βᵢβⱼσₘ² ⇒ ρᵢⱼ = βᵢβⱼσₘ² / (σᵢσⱼ) = ρᵢₘ · ρⱼₘ, where ρᵢₘ = βᵢσₘ/σᵢ

If ρᵢⱼ = ρ* for every pair, then ρᵢₘρⱼₘ = ρ* for all i, j. Taking any three stocks i, j, k shows ρᵢₘ = ρⱼₘ = ρₖₘ, so every stock has the same correlation with the market, ρᵢₘ = √ρ*. Therefore

βᵢ = √ρ* · σᵢ / σₘ

单指数模型下 ρᵢⱼ = ρᵢₘρⱼₘ。所有 ρᵢⱼ 都等于 ρ* 时，每只股票与市场的相关系数都为 √ρ*，所以 βᵢ = √ρ*·σᵢ/σₘ。

Three orthogonal indexes三个正交指数

Complete the procedure in Appendix A for reducing a general three-index model to a three-index model with orthogonal indexes.

Start with Rᵢ = aᵢ* + bᵢ₁*I₁* + bᵢ₂*I₂* + bᵢ₃*I₃* + cᵢ, where the starred indexes may be correlated.

1Keep the first index保留第一个指数
I₁ = I₁*
2Remove I₁ from the second index从第二个指数中剔除 I₁
Regress I₂* = γ₀ + γ₁I₁ + d₂ ⇒ I₂ ≡ d₂ = I₂* − γ₀ − γ₁I₁ (uncorrelated with I₁)
3Remove I₁ and I₂ from the third index从第三个指数中剔除 I₁ 和 I₂

4Substitute back代回原式
I₂* = γ₀ + γ₁I₁ + I₂ I₃* = θ₀ + θ₁I₁ + θ₂I₂ + I₃ Rᵢ = [aᵢ* + bᵢ₂*γ₀ + bᵢ₃*θ₀] + [bᵢ₁* + bᵢ₂*γ₁ + bᵢ₃*θ₁] · I₁ + [bᵢ₂* + bᵢ₃*θ₂] · I₂ + bᵢ₃* · I₃ + cᵢ

Properties of utility functionsp.247–249 (App.)效用函数的性质

Nonsatiation: U′(W) > 0. Risk aversion: U″(W) < 0 (reject a fair gamble); U″ = 0 is risk neutral, U″ > 0 risk seeking. Absolute risk aversion A(W) = −U″/U′ says how the dollar amount put in risky assets changes with wealth (decreasing A is the usual assumption). Relative risk aversion R(W) = W·A(W) says how the percentage in risky assets changes; ln W has constant relative risk aversion. Quadratic utility W − bW² has increasing absolute risk aversion and is only sensible for W < 1/(2b).

非饱和 U′ > 0；风险厌恶 U″ < 0。绝对风险厌恶 A(W) = −U″/U′ 描述投入风险资产的金额如何随财富变化；相对风险厌恶 R(W) = W·A(W) 描述比例的变化。ln W 是常数相对风险厌恶。二次效用只在 W < 1/(2b) 时合理。

A(W) = −U″(W)/U′(W) R(W) = W·A(W)

Safety-first criteriap.226–232安全第一准则

Focus on bad outcomes. With normally distributed returns each reduces to a rule in mean–standard deviation space. Roy: minimize Prob(Rₚ < RL), i.e. maximize (R̄ₚ − RL)/σₚ. Kataoka: maximize the lower limit RL subject to Prob(Rₚ ≤ RL) ≤ α, i.e. maximize R̄ₚ − zσₚ. Telser: maximize R̄ₚ subject to Prob(Rₚ ≤ RL) ≤ α, i.e. R̄ₚ − zσₚ ≥ RL. For α = 5%, z = 1.65; for α = 10%, z = 1.28.

Roy：最小化跌破 RL 的概率，即最大化 (R̄ − RL)/σ。Kataoka：在跌破概率 ≤ α 的约束下最大化下限。Telser：在同样约束下最大化期望收益。α = 5% 时 z = 1.65，α = 10% 时 z = 1.28。

14.9

Leaving bonds out of the market index市场指数中漏掉债券

As we will see in the next chapter, most tests of the CAPM involve tests on common stock data and perform the tests using the S&P index. You have just had a revelation that bonds are also marketable assets and thus should belong in the market return. Show what effect leaving them out might have on stocks with different characteristics.

Show answer 查看答案

The true market is M = w·S + (1 − w)·B (stocks plus bonds), so a stock’s true beta depends on its covariance with both: βᵢ = [w·cov(Rᵢ, S) + (1 − w)·cov(Rᵢ, B)]/σₘ². Measuring beta against the S&P alone ignores the bond term.

  • Interest-rate-sensitive stocks (utilities, banks, high-dividend stocks) covary strongly with bonds. Their true beta is higher, relative to other stocks, than their S&P beta suggests, so their true expected return is higher than the S&P-based SML predicts. In tests they will appear to have positive alphas.
  • Stocks with little bond covariance (e.g. growth stocks) are the reverse: relative to their S&P beta they will appear to have negative alphas.
  • The fitted SML is distorted: its intercept and slope no longer equal RF and R̄ₘ − RF, and the test may wrongly reject (or accept) the CAPM. This is Roll’s point in Chapter 15: a test is only a test of whether the chosen proxy is efficient.

真正的市场包含债券。与债券高度相关的股票（公用事业、银行、高股息股）真实 beta 被低估，检验中会显示正 alpha；与债券无关的股票会显示负 alpha。整条 SML 被扭曲，这正是 Roll 批评的要点。

Empirical Tests of Equilibrium Models均衡模型的实证检验

Key concepts本章要点

Ex ante model, ex post testsp.340–341事前模型，事后检验

The CAPM is about expectations, but we only observe realized returns. Tests assume expectations are on average right, so the ex post version is Rᵢₜ = RFₜ + βᵢ(Rₘₜ − RFₜ) + εᵢₜ. In any single period the market can do worse than RF, and then high-beta stocks should do worse. Tests need long periods.

CAPM 讲的是预期，检验只能用已实现收益。单期内市场可能跑输 RF，这时高 beta 股票本来就应该更差，所以要用长时间数据。

Two-pass tests and what they findp.341–352两步法检验及结论

First pass: time-series regressions estimate each security’s (or portfolio’s) beta. Second pass: cross-sectional regression of average returns on beta: R̄ᵢ = γ₀ + γ₁βᵢ + γ₂βᵢ² + γ₃σ²(eᵢ) + ηᵢ. Roll’s critiquep.356–358Roll 的批评

The linear relation between expected return and beta holds exactly, as a mathematical identity, whenever betas are measured against any mean–variance efficient portfolio. 15.3

If M is not efficient, the zero-beta CAPM failsM 无效时零 beta CAPM 不成立

Show that if the market portfolio is not an efficient portfolio, then R̄ᵢ = R̄Z + βᵢ(R̄ₘ − R̄Z) cannot in general hold.

Show answer 查看答案

Write the relation for all N assets at once in vector form, with μ the vector of expected returns, Σ the covariance matrix, xₘ the market weights and 1 a vector of ones. Since βᵢ = (Σxₘ)ᵢ/σₘ²:

μ = R̄_Z·1 + [(R̄ₘ − R̄_Z)/σₘ²] · Σ·xₘ ⇒ Σ·xₘ = [σₘ²/(R̄ₘ − R̄_Z)] · (μ − R̄_Z·1) ⇒ xₘ = k · Σ⁻¹(μ − R̄_Z·1), k a positive constant

But x = k·Σ⁻¹(μ − c·1) is exactly the first-order condition for the tangency portfolio from a rate c: the portfolio that maximizes (R̄ₚ − c)/σₚ, which lies on the efficient frontier. So if the relation holds for every asset, M must be on the efficient frontier. Contrapositive: if M is not efficient, no R̄Z can make the relation hold for all assets.

把关系写成向量形式可推出 xₘ ∝ Σ⁻¹(μ − R̄Z·1)，这正是有效组合（切点组合）的一阶条件。所以关系对所有资产成立 ⇔ M 有效；M 无效时关系不可能普遍成立。这就是 Roll 批评的数学基础。

The equilibrium model gives the return a manager should earn for the risk taken; performance is the difference between actual and required return.

Key concepts本章要点

Predictive realization diagram (PRD)p.702–704预测–实现图

Plot the predicted change in earnings (P, horizontal) against the realized change (R, vertical). Points on the 45° line are perfect forecasts. Quadrants I and III are correct direction; II and IV are turning-point errors (wrong direction). In quadrant I, points below the line mean the forecaster overestimated increases.

横轴为预测变化，纵轴为实际变化。45° 线上为完美预测；第二、四象限是方向判断错误；第一象限线下方表示高估了增长。

Mean squared forecast error and Theil’s coefficientp.701–702均方预测误差与 Theil 系数

Pᵢ = predicted change = forecast − previous earnings; Rᵢ = realized change = actual − previous earnings. MSFE is the same whether computed on levels or changes. Theil’s inequality coefficient compares the forecaster with a naive “no change” forecast: TIC = 0 is perfect, TIC = 1 is no better than predicting no change, TIC > 1 is worse.

用盈利变化计算：MSFE 对水平或变化都一样。TIC 与“不变”预测比较：0 完美，1 与不变预测一样，大于 1 更差。

MSFE = (1/N) Σ (Pᵢ − Rᵢ)² TIC = Σ (Pᵢ − Rᵢ)² / Σ Rᵢ²

Decomposing forecast errorp.704–708预测误差分解

By level of aggregation: economy-wide error, industry error (misjudging how industries differ from the average), and firm error (misjudging firms within an industry). Consensus forecasts: almost all error is random and firm-specific.

Portfolio Management Revisited再论投资组合管理

How the theory of the book shows up in real management styles, for stocks and bonds.

Key concepts本章要点

Passive management and index fundsp.715–718被动管理与指数基金

Passive = trading on mechanical rules from past data, with no forecasts. The standard CAPM justifies holding the market (approximated by an index such as the S&P 500); in practice index funds beat most active managers after costs. Three ways to build one, trading tracking error against transaction costs: (1) hold every stock at index weight, (2) optimize a smaller set of stocks to track the index historically, (3) match the index on characteristics (sector, industry, size). Variants based on other models: yield-tilted funds (posttax CAPM), small-cap and low-P/E funds (anomalies), and funds holding T-bills plus index futures.

被动管理：按机械规则，不做预测。CAPM 支持持有市场组合；实际中指数基金扣费后胜过多数主动经理。三种构建方法在跟踪误差和交易成本之间取舍。还有股息倾斜、小盘股、低市盈率等变体。

Active managementp.718–720主动管理

Any departure from the passive benchmark weights is a bet based on a forecast. Three styles: market timers change portfolio beta according to market forecasts (via futures, options or the cash/bond mix); sector selectors over- or underweight industries or sectors; security selectors pick individual mispriced stocks, often using the single-index framework to trade off alpha against residual risk.

偏离基准权重就是基于预测的押注。三种风格：择时（调整 beta）、行业选择（超配或低配行业）、个股选择。

See alsoCh 26 timing measuresCh 27 evaluating forecasts

International diversification, bonds and liabilitiesp.720–728国际分散、债券管理与负债匹配

International portfolios add a choice of countries and currency exposure on top of the domestic decisions. Bond portfolios can be passive (bond index funds) or active (forecasting interest rates, credit spreads, sector swaps). When the manager must meet a stream of liabilities (pension funds, insurers), the problem becomes managing the surplus (assets minus liabilities): immunization and duration matching protect against interest-rate shifts, and the risky asset mix is chosen relative to the liabilities rather than in isolation.

国际组合多了国家和汇率选择。债券组合可以被动或主动。需要偿付负债时，目标变成管理盈余（资产减负债），用免疫和久期匹配对冲利率风险。
